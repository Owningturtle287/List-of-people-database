World storage

This app reads the canonical world from GitHub. Your phone edits stay in local storage until you export and share them. When a newer published snapshot appears, you can review it before replacing local changes.

Install on iPhone

Open the published site in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Export backups regularly; browser storage can be cleared by iOS.

Simulation boundaries

Procedural activity is a lightweight deterministic model, not an AI service. It never automatically rescues or kills people, invents resources, or advances the clock. Major events, weather, medical outcomes and custom character decisions are directed by the story controller. The map is schematic, not real geography.
